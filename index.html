Everything you need to print a book

A focused toolkit for turning writing into something you can hold.

  • ↴

    Markdown support

    Write in Markdown — headings, lists, footnotes and GFM syntax — and let PrintFold flow it across pages automatically.

  • ⊞

    Booklet editing

    Signature-based imposition with fold and cut marks, multi-row sheets, and a visual spread editor for fine-tuning every page.

  • Aa

    Custom fonts

    Drop in your own .ttf, .otf or .woff files, or reach for system and Google Fonts — all embedded straight into your PDF.

  • ⟲

    Reflow options

    Adjust margins, typography and spacing and watch text reflow live. H1s start on a recto, footnotes settle into place, and everything repaginates instantly.

  • ⬡

    Static spreads & regions

    Add covers, images and shapes, or pour text through custom polygon regions that wrap to any silhouette.

  • ⤓

    PDF export

    Export a print-ready PDF with embedded fonts — as a folded booklet, double-sided pages, or single-sided sheets.

From draft to finished print

1

Bring your Markdown

Start a project and add one or more .md files. They flow together in order.

2

Shape the layout

Pick fonts, set margins, design covers, and tweak the booklet settings until it looks right.

3

Export & fold

Generate an imposed PDF, print duplex, fold along the marks, and bind your signatures.
